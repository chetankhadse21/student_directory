Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Go to the Login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.
Why is my page not loading?
Check your internet connection and refresh the page. If the issue persists, try pressing Ctrl+Shift+R to hard reload and clear cached files.
Where can I find assignments?
Go to the Home page → select your semester → click the "Assignments" category button. All assignments for your semester will appear below.
Can I download notes and books?
Yes! Click any resource card to open it. You'll see a preview and a download button to save the PDF to your device.
How do I change the theme (dark/light)?
Click the 🌙 moon icon in the top-right of the header. The theme preference is saved automatically in your browser.
